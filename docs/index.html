DISCOVERY & CLASSIFICATION

Understand what exists before changing anything

Large Dynatrace environments often contain thousands of entities with uneven tagging, ownership, and classification. The platform makes those gaps visible before migration or optimization work begins.

  • Entity and schema discovery
  • Tag and property inventory
  • Ownership and criticality coverage
  • Business-service and service-offering mapping

TOPOLOGY INTELLIGENCE

Inspect every entity in context

Drill into hosts, services, process groups, and related entities while preserving the metadata and topology needed to explain how the environment is connected.

  • Entity context and properties
  • Related hosts, services and process groups
  • Incoming and outgoing relationships
  • Classification source and evidence
